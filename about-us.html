Welcome to the Golden City

Explore Jaisalmer at Your Own Pace

Jaisalmer is known for golden sandstone architecture, historic streets, havelis, desert landscapes and memorable sunset views. KK Tours & Travel helps visitors enquire about private taxi journeys and sightseeing around the city and nearby destinations.

Every trip begins with the details you share: your dates, pickup point, passenger count and the places you hope to visit. We discuss the route, taxi availability and fare before you confirm your plan.

We aim to make travel enquiries straightforward, with contact by phone or WhatsApp so you can review the details directly.
